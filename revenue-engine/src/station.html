What you are looking at

Room = one money path. Lit means active. Amber beacons and hazard stripes mean gated. Red emergency light means killed.
Six desks = the six stages. A green screen means the stage target is met. A blinking blue screen marks the next stage. The dots on the desk edge are progress.
Crates = evidenced outcomes logged on that path, one crate each (display capped at 16).
Reactor tube = spend against the path budget. It burns orange past 50% and red past 80%.
Crew = a real agent run. No run, no crew member. Walking in is a run start; walking out is a run end, carrying a crate if the run logged outcomes.
Vault = evidenced revenue. One gold bar per ladder rung crossed ($1, $1K, $10K, $100K, $1M); the hologram shows your Commander level.
Posters = posts you published, logged with their live URL. A gold corner means that post has earned money.
Outbox dock = one sheet per draft an agent wrote. Nothing is sent without you.
Pulses in the halls = a live agent run on that path, reporting back to Command. They stop when the run ends.
Gold figure = you, the Commander. Decoration: wall screens, rugs, chairs, mugs, plants, racks, windows, solar wings and everything in space are scenery with no meaning.
Click any room (floor, wall or label), the vault for the station roster, or the outbox dock.
