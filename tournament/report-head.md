# Top 10 Money Tournament, September 2026

Joshua asked: think outside the box, pick the top 10 most likely to make the most money, only after proving every other idea lesser; believe in every idea until it reveals itself lesser; a spread of types: trends, things that have been around forever, and unsolved problems solved efficiently.

**Method.** 144 raw ideas from 12 lenses (trend scout, been-around-forever, unsolved-efficiency, contrarian-boring, Ark-La-Tex local, employment paths, solo software, macro shifts, asset-based, content/media, B2B services, where-money-hides) merged into 109 distinct ideas. Each got a dossier (median net income at about 24 months, top decile, probability of reaching the median, months to first dollar, capital, durability to 2031, openness, fit) built from the generators' sourced claims plus 30 verification searches, and a skeptic line. Three independent judges ranked all 109 under different lenses (expected value; durability and ceiling; speed, capital and execution odds), aggregated by Borda count. The top 10 had to satisfy spread rules: at least 2 trend, 2 evergreen, 2 unsolved; no two in the same category; at least one employment path; at least five businesses. Then ranks 11 to 30 each got a refereed head-to-head against the incumbent they would displace (21 decisions), and the two picks that were the same business under two labels were refereed against each other. Ranks 31 to 109 are eliminated on the panel's unanimous placement plus the skeptic's evidence; they were not individually refereed, and that cap is stated here rather than hidden. The original design (one research agent, one skeptic and one referee per idea) was refused at the account's usage limit twice after ~700K tokens each time, so the evidence stage ran in-session from shared authoritative sources instead.

**Assumptions.** Market-general question, scored for a motivated adult who could be earning within 24 months without a new 4-year degree, with about $50K of accessible capital unless stated. Fit for Joshua (call-center marketing ops leader who ships software solo with AI) counted in one judge of three, so four of the ten are call-center-adjacent; the market-general alternates sit in the near misses.

## The 10

**1. Outbound contact-rate and TCPA compliance managed service for lead-gen call centers.** Unsolved problem, business. Median at 2 years about $130,000; top decile $600,000; capital $5,000; first dollar in about 2 months. The money: outbound centers lose roughly half their answers to spam labels and face a 50-state mini-TCPA patchwork with $500 to $1,500 per-call statutory damages; a boutique that runs DID reputation, branded calling, litigator scrubs and the Texas SB 140 desk bills $2,000 to $4,000 a month per client, and the tooling vendors (Numeracle, Caller ID Reputation) sell software, not operations. Why it won: highest Borda total, first for the execution judge, held against the dialer-migration practice and the aged-lead AI SDR in refereed head-to-heads. Skeptic's best shot: attribution of contact-rate lift, carrier policy keeps changing, and clients must be found outside his employer. It survived because the recurring retainer is evidence-backed and the product already exists in the form of his DID dashboard and Convoso playbook. Efficient way in: five lead-gen shops on a $3,000 retainer with a 60-day contact-rate guarantee. Fit 10 of 10.

**2. Recurring-route pest control company built to 80% contract revenue.** Evergreen, business. Median $100,000 (solo take-home $80,000 to $140,000 by year two per OnCallClerk and FieldRoutes); top $200,000; capital $40,000; probability 0.6, the highest in the top 10; first dollar in 2 months. Sources: fieldroutes.com and oncallclerk.com. Why it won: second on Borda, second for the expected-value judge, and it held against the Medicare Advantage book, the property-tax firm, fire inspection routes and home care in head-to-heads, each time on probability (0.6 vs 0.4) and months to first dollar. Skeptic: route density and a Texas Department of Agriculture applicator license are the gates; metros are crowded. Survived because the Ark-La-Tex is not a metro and the numbers held on search. Efficient way in: license in 2 to 3 months, 200 quarterly accounts at $120 to $150 per visit is $100,000-plus recurring; build to 80% contract revenue and the exit multiples are cited (6.5 to 8x EBITDA). Fit 7.

**3. Fractional head of contact center and revenue operations for PE-backed multi-location healthcare.** Trend, employment path. Median $150,000 (two retainers at $8,000 to $15,000 a month); top $400,000; capital $0; first dollar in 3 months. Why it won: third on Borda, in every judge's top 8, held against per-diem nurse staffing. Skeptic: relationship-sold, slow ramp without a PE network, competes with bigger-name ex-executives. Survived because the median is the highest reachable income in the pool for his exact resume, at zero capital. Efficient way in: one retainer, sold on a 90-day book-rate or show-rate turnaround with his current numbers as the case study. Fit 10.

**4. Roll-off dumpster rental fleet with online self-booking.** Evergreen, business. Median $130,000 (one-truck owner earnings $120,000 to $250,000; $350,000 to $450,000 revenue with a driver); top $300,000; capital about $90,000 (used hook-lift truck plus cans); payback 18 to 24 months. Sources: curbwaste.com and quipli.com. Why it won: first for the expected-value judge, fourth on Borda, held against estate cleanouts, septic pumping, forestry mulching and septic installation, every time on median and capital efficiency. Skeptic: tipping fees, truck downtime, national haulers' pricing. Survived because it is the best-documented cash business at this capital level. Efficient way in: one truck, 15 cans, self-booking site, contractor accounts along I-30 and storm cleanup. Fit 6.

**5. Enterprise account executive selling AI voice and chat agents into contact centers.** Trend, employment path. Median $180,000 (enterprise AE median OTE $280,000 on 12,819 RepVue submissions; a first AI-vendor role more likely lands at $150,000 to $200,000); top $500,000-plus (RepVue top performers $677,000); capital $0. Source: repvue.com. Why it won: fifth on Borda, held against roofing sales. Skeptic: enterprise AE hiring wants a closing record, so the realistic first seat is mid-market at $120,000 to $160,000 OTE. Survived because even the skeptic's number beats most of the pool, and his contact-center domain is the hook AI vendors lack. Efficient way in: target vendors selling into call centers (voice AI, agent-assist, compliance) where he has run the buyer's job. Fit 7.

**6. Buy a retiring owner's trades shop with SBA 7(a) money, modernize marketing and dispatch, exit to a PE platform.** Evergreen, hybrid. Median $150,000 (median deal about $350,000 on $165,000 seller's discretionary earnings; HVAC median sale price $800,000); top $1.5 million-plus (owner shops trade at 2 to 3.5x SDE, $1M-plus EBITDA shops clear 5 to 10x, Champions Group went at 18.5x in February 2026); capital $80,000 to $160,000 down plus a personal guarantee; 6 to 18 months to close. Sources: bizbuysell.com valuation benchmarks and dealprospectors.com. Why it won: first for the durability judge, third for expected value, and the only card whose ceiling is a PE multiple on a durable, sellable asset; it held against the public-adjusting firm. Skeptic: down payment above the stated capital, search time, and the exit needs $1M-plus EBITDA. Survived because the arbitrage is real and the missing ingredient in most small shops (phones, marketing, dispatch) is his day job. Efficient way in: owners over 60 in 5,000 to 30,000-person Ark-La-Tex towns; SBA lender pre-qualification first. Fit 8.

**7. Forward-deployed AI engineer or AI implementation contractor for voice and contact-center AI vendors.** Trend, hybrid. Median $170,000 (FDE median base $190,000 to $207,500; mid-level total comp $180,000 to $260,000; postings up about 800%); top $400,000; capital $0; 3 months. Sources: levels.fyi and recruitingfromscratch.com. Why it won: seventh on Borda, held against the spreadsheet-to-app studio and mobile RV tech. Skeptic: technical interviews screen for engineering depth, not AI-assisted shipping. Survived because the 1099 implementation path at $100 to $200 an hour clears the median without the interview gate. Efficient way in: contract deployments for voice-AI vendors going into call centers; portfolio is the shipped apps plus dialer integrations. Fit 7.

**8. Show-rate guarantee desk: performance-priced no-show reduction and dormant-patient reactivation for high-ticket consult businesses.** Unsolved problem, business. Median $120,000; top $500,000; capital $10,000; 2 months. The money: med spas, dental-implant and ortho centers, LASIK, solar and roofing replacement sales lose 25 to 40% of booked consults; a desk paid per recovered show. Why it won: eighth on Borda and the busiest incumbent in the challenge round, holding against the book-rate desk (refereed as the same business), the B2B collections agency, senior-living placement and the denial-recovery shop. Skeptic: attribution disputes on performance pricing and AI confirmation bots thinning the labor moat. Survived because the performance model removes buyer risk and the show-rate playbook (the 11-component benchmark) already exists. Fit 10.

**9. Cost-segregation study firm riding permanent 100% bonus depreciation.** Trend, business. Median $120,000 (15 studies a year at $8,000); top $1 million; capital $10,000; 4 months. The money: studies bill $5,000 to $13,000 for residential and small multifamily and $12,000 to $60,000 for commercial, and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act made 100% bonus depreciation permanent for property placed in service after January 19, 2025, so every acquisition needs the substantiating study. Source: baselane.com. Why it won: the tournament's one upset. It sat 14th on Borda and beat the generator load-bank service in a refereed head-to-head on cited per-study pricing, a cited permanent statutory driver, a $1 million ceiling against $350,000, and $10,000 of capital against $80,000. Skeptic: engineering credibility, national firms, and $99 AI estimates compressing the small end. Survived because the engineering can be subcontracted and the report engine is buildable. Efficient way in: partner with a licensed engineer, build the takeoff and report tool, sell through CPAs and short-term-rental investors. Fit 6.

**10. Diesel generator load-bank testing and fuel polishing for data centers, hospitals and telecom sites.** Trend, business. Median $110,000; top $350,000; capital $80,000; 4 months. The money: NFPA 110 and Joint Commission rules require annual load-bank tests and clean fuel on every backup generator, each visit bills $1,500 to $6,000, and the data-center buildout adds thousands of new units. Why it is here: tenth on Borda and in two judges' top 10; it lost only to cost segregation, then refilled the slot freed when the book-rate desk collapsed into the show-rate desk, because it outranked every remaining non-loser and held against ADAS calibration. Skeptic: the per-visit numbers are generator-sourced, and it presumes generator or electrical competence he does not have. Survives with the weakest evidence in the ten; a verified book of contracts would move it up, a failed hire of a generator tech would move it out. Fit 5.

## The spread

Types: unsolved 2 (contact-rate service, show-rate desk), evergreen 3 (pest control, dumpsters, trades-shop acquisition), trend 5 (fractional exec, enterprise AE, FDE contractor, cost segregation, load-bank testing). Forms: 2 employment paths, 2 hybrids, 6 businesses. Ten distinct categories. The spread rules cost no money: the pure Borda top 10 already satisfied them, and the only non-Borda moves were the duplicate collapse and the one upset. The honest concentration risk is that four of ten sell into call centers or AI vendors; if that world is closed to him by a non-compete or conflict, the near misses below are the replacements.

## Upsets and what changed

One upset: cost segregation over generator load-bank testing. One collapse: the book-rate desk and the show-rate desk are the same business, so the show-rate desk kept the slot and load-bank testing refilled the freed one. No completeness-critic round was run, for budget; obvious paths never in the pool and therefore untested are a property-and-casualty insurance agency book, a bookkeeping and tax firm, commercial cleaning, mortgage origination, and government-contracting resale.

## What would flip this

1. If he cannot sell to call centers outside his employer, the contact-rate service and show-rate desk drop out and the Medicare Advantage book and property-tax firm move in.
2. If $80,000 to $160,000 of down payment is not available, the trades-shop acquisition falls to a pest-control or dumpster route he builds instead of buys.
3. If AI-vendor hiring cools in 2027, both employment paths (enterprise AE, FDE) weaken and fire inspection routes and cost segregation rise.
4. A verified contract book for load-bank testing restores it above cost segregation; a failed generator-tech hire removes it.
5. Every dossier number is a 2025-2026 snapshot; the durable claims are the mandated-demand ones (NFPA, CMS, OBBBA, TDA licensing), and those are the ones the ten lean on hardest.
