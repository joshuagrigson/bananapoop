The spread

Unsolved 2, evergreen 3, trend 5. Two employment paths, two hybrids, six businesses, ten distinct categories. The spread rules cost nothing: the pure Borda top 10 already met them. The one concentration risk is that four picks sell into call centers or AI vendors; if that lane closes, the Medicare Advantage renewal book, the Texas property-tax protest firm and fire and life-safety inspection routes are the replacements, and each lost its head-to-head narrowly.

What changed in the challenge round

  • Upset: cost segregation (rank 14) beat generator load-bank testing (rank 10) on cited per-study pricing, a permanent statutory driver, a $1M ceiling and $10K of capital against $80K.
  • Collapse: the book-rate desk for PE home-services platforms and the show-rate desk were refereed as the same business; the show-rate desk led on every number and kept the slot.
  • Load-bank testing refilled the freed slot because it outranked every remaining idea that had not lost a head-to-head; it holds the weakest evidence in the ten.
  • Not run, for budget: a completeness-critic round. Paths never in the pool and therefore untested: a property-and-casualty agency book, a bookkeeping and tax firm, commercial cleaning, mortgage origination, government-contracting resale.

What would flip this

  • No access to call-center clients outside the employer: the contact-rate service and show-rate desk drop, the Medicare Advantage book and property-tax firm move in.
  • No $80,000 to $160,000 down payment: the trades-shop acquisition becomes a pest-control or dumpster route built from scratch.
  • AI-vendor hiring cools in 2027: both employment paths weaken; fire inspection routes and cost segregation rise.
  • A verified contract book for load-bank testing restores it above cost segregation; a failed generator-tech hire removes it.
