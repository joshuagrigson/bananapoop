## 1. Power out tonight? 5 rules (pah-power-out.mp4, 46 s)

```
If the power goes out tonight, these 5 rules matter more than anything in your pantry 👇

1. Generator at least 20 feet from the house. Never in the garage, even with the door open.
2. Grills and camp stoves stay outside. Never heat the house with the oven.
3. A battery carbon monoxide alarm outside every sleeping area. Test it before the storm.
4. Too cold inside? Go somewhere warm. In the 2021 Texas freeze, the cold itself took the most lives.
5. Keep the fridge shut. About 4 hours for the fridge, about 48 for a full freezer, 24 for a half-full one.

Save this before the next ice storm. Share it with the person in your family who never plans ahead.
```

## 2. Bleach drops per gallon (pah-bleach-water.mp4)

```
Boil notice, or no water at all? Here's exactly how much bleach makes a gallon safe to drink 👇

• Plain, unscented household bleach only. Not scented, color-safe or splash-less.
• 5–6% bleach: 8 drops per gallon.
• 8.25% bleach: 6 drops per gallon.
• Cloudy water: let it settle, strain it through a clean cloth, then double the drops.
• Stir, wait 30 minutes. It should smell faintly of bleach. No smell? Repeat and wait 15 more.

Amounts from the CDC and EPA. Screenshot it and tape it inside a cabinet door.
```

## 5. No room for 20 feet? (pah-no-twenty-feet.mp4, 49 s)

```
The rule says run a generator 20 feet from the house. But what if your yard isn't that big? 👇

• As far from the house as your yard allows, out in the open. Never under a porch or carport, never in the garage.
• Point the exhaust away from your house and your neighbor's. Close the windows and doors on that side.
• Carbon monoxide alarms inside: battery, or plug-in with a battery backup. If one goes off, shut the generator down and get everyone outside.
• Buying one? Get carbon monoxide auto-shutoff. Look for UL 2201 or PGMA G300 on the box.
• No yard at all? Skip the gas generator. A battery power station has no exhaust and is safe indoors. Or head to a warming center.

Share this with the friend in the apartment or the townhouse.
```
