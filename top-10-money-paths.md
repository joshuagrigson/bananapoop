# Top 10 Money Paths — September 2026

**Question:** Out of every idea, which 10 are most likely to make the most money in the market as it is right now? The 10 should be a spread of trend plays, businesses that have been around forever, and plays that solve unsolved problems efficiently.

**Method:** I started with 65 candidates. Each one kept its place until a survivor beat it on a specific number: the market is shrinking, the margin is thin, the odds are too low, or the barrier is too high. Each cut below names which survivor beats it and why. When a cut rests on my judgment rather than data, it's tagged **[judgment]**. "Proven" here means *beaten by a named survivor on a stated measure*. It does not mean mathematically certain.

**Scoring:** expected value = typical annual income in years 3–5 × the odds of getting there. After that I weighed tailwind (demand growth vs. supply), how durable the idea is against AI and competitors, how fast the first dollar arrives, and how much capital it needs. Income and odds figures are **derived estimates**. The market figures come from sources and are cited at the bottom.

## The market as it is (Sept 2026)

- On 9/16 the Fed **raised** rates to 3.75–4.00%, its first hike in more than 3 years. The 30-year mortgage is **7.03%**. CPI is 3.4%, and energy is up 16.3% y/y because of the Iran war and oil.
- Recent college graduates face **5.6%** unemployment and 42% underemployment. Entry-level postings are down 7.5% y/y while senior postings are up 14.7%.
- Hyperscalers (the biggest cloud and AI companies) will spend **$720–745B** on capex in 2026, up 77%. Debt now funds 32% of that, up from 9%.
- Only **17–20%** of firms use AI in production (Census). Upwork client spend on "AI integration" rose **178%**.
- About **11,400 Americans turn 65 every day**.
- The residential solar credit expired 12/31/25. Rooftop solar is down about 18% and home batteries are down 27%.
- Local: **Project Big Pine** is a planned **$3.5B, 500-acre data-center campus in Bowie County**, which is Texarkana.

### Why each one survived

**1. Buy a boring service business (SBA 7(a)).** This is the only path where year-1 income is already six figures, because you buy cash flow instead of building it.
- HVAC: median SDE (owner pay plus profit) is **$315,225** on a median sale price of about **$800K**, a 24.8% margin. Plumbing: median sale price $837,500 at about **2.24x SDE**.
- Private equity pays about **9.5x EBITDA** for HVAC platforms. Buying at roughly 2.5x and selling into a roll-up is multiple arbitrage, the oldest trick in the book.
- Rough math: $720K borrowed at about 9.75% over 10 years is about $113K/yr in debt service. That's 2.8x coverage, above the new **1.25x minimum** that takes effect 10/1/26.
- Risks: overpaying, key technicians walking out, and SBA volume down 20.9% in FY26. Pest control (Rollins made 26 acquisitions in 2025), septic, and towing (SDE $270K at 3.5x) are all good targets inside this path.

**2. Vertical AI, outcome-priced.** Generalist "ChatGPT + Zapier" agencies are crowded. Vertical offers where you're paid on outcomes are still open.
- 80% of firms still aren't using AI in production. Retell AI hit $60M ARR, up 650%.
- Clearest gap: mid-market contact-center QA. Observe.AI runs about $60–180K per year per 100 seats, with **100-seat minimums**, so centers with 10–200 seats are priced out.
- Example: 15 centers × 40 seats × $60/seat/month = **$432K ARR**.
- ★ Joshua: the grading-and-coaching platform already exists. See the IP warning below.

**3. Home care, private-pay.**
- About 11,400 people turn 65 every day. Aide jobs are projected to grow 18%.
- The median agency does **$2.3M revenue, up 14%**. Franchise units run $1.2–2.8M median.
- Federal Medicaid cuts of more than $1T mean private-pay clients are the safer revenue.
- Hard part: caregiver turnover of 75–79%. In Texas the agency needs an HHSC license (HCSSA).
- The owner-margin estimate is **[judgment]**. I didn't verify it in this pass.

**4. Data-center / grid electrician or lineman.** This has the highest odds of any path.
- The US needs **130K more electricians** by 2030 just for AI infrastructure.
- Linemen: median **$95,320**, top 10% over $128,690.
- Traveling data-center electricians reportedly make $130–170K with overtime. That figure comes from recruiter blogs and isn't verified.
- Project Big Pine is building in Bowie County.
- It can lead to an electrical contracting business (SDE of about $320K).

**5. Enterprise account executive.** Enterprise AEs have a median OTE (on-target earnings) of **$280K**, but only 41% hit quota. That figure is a RepVue snippet and isn't verified. You need zero capital. AI companies are hiring sellers because AI capex is surging. The way in is SDR/BDR (outbound prospecting roles), which fit a call-center sales background.

**6. Texas property-tax protest, AI-run.**
- About **3M protests are filed a year**. Only **22%** of homeowners have ever appealed.
- Firms charge **25–50% of the savings**. Ownwell's average saving is $774, and its customer count grew 180% in 2025.
- Headwind: **Prop 13 (2025)** raised the homestead exemption to $140K. So point it at rentals, land and small commercial property.
- You must register with TDLR (the state licensing agency) as a property tax consultant.

**7. Roofing / storm restoration.**
- Texas home premiums rose 60% from 2019 to 2024, and insurers are using aerial imagery to non-renew homes.
- Roofing businesses show SDE of about **$389K on $2M revenue** (listing data, flagged). Texas has no state roofing license.
- It's a sales-driven trade, so call-center skills transfer.
- Public adjusters are capped at 10% in Texas, so treat claims help as a skill inside this path, not a separate business.

**8. Provider-side denial recovery.**
- ACA plans deny 19% of in-network claims, and **fewer than 1% are appealed**.
- Medicare Advantage prior-auth denials are overturned **80.7%** of the time when appealed, but only 11.5% get appealed.
- Hospitals spend $25.7B a year fighting denials. Small practices can't afford the enterprise revenue-cycle firms.
- Providers pay out of money you recover. Patients pay about $40–50 or nothing, which is why patient-side services lost this slot.

**9. Call-reputation fixer.**
- **86%** of calls from unknown numbers go unanswered.
- At Numeracle, businesses seeking protection rose **46%** and remediations **44%** (2025).
- The problem stays unsolved because each major carrier's labels come from a different opaque analytics engine (First Orion, TNS, TransUnion), and they don't share a single appeal channel.
- Pricing runs $250–8,000/month, but that range is unverified.
- ★ Joshua: the DID Dashboard with its early-warning radar is already built.
- The load-bearing risk is the FCC's proposed rule requiring carriers to pass and display verified caller name and logo (Rich Call Data). If it lands, this market could shrink.

**10. CRNA.** Median **$236,590**, the highest verified non-MD pay on the list, with 10% growth. The path is long: BSN, RN, a year of ICU, then a 3-year doctorate. It ranks last only because the payoff comes after the 5-year window.

## Everything else, and what beats it

**Gate A. Market shrinking or rate-crushed now**
- Residential solar installer: installs down 12% in Q2, forecast down 33% in 2026, 25D credit gone. Loses to **#4** (same skills, booming side).
- Home battery installer: down 27% y/y. Loses to **#4**.
- EV charger install: federal EV credit ended 9/30/25 (my knowledge, not re-verified this pass). Loses to **#4**.
- Real estate agent: NAR membership falling. Median gross is $59.2K, and agents in their first 2 years made $8K. Loses to **#5** (same selling skill, about 4x the pay).
- Mortgage broker: rates are 7.03% and the Fed just hiked. Loses to **#5**.
- Rental real estate / ADU builder: 7% debt against 5–7.5% cap rates is negative leverage. Loses to **#1** (buy cash flow at 2.5x instead).
- Self-storage: rents down 1.6% y/y, hangover from overbuilding. Loses to **#1**.
- Car wash: Texas/DFW is among the most oversupplied markets, new builds cost $4–8M, and Zips went through Chapter 11. Loses to **#1**.
- Travel nursing: pay 42% below the pandemic peak, industry growing 1%. Loses to **#10**.
- GLP-1 clinic: TrumpRx cash prices of $149–349 and an FDA crackdown on compounding. Loses to **#3** (aging demand without the price collapse).
- Funeral home: 63.4% cremation rate erodes revenue per call. Loses to **#3**.
- Trucking owner-operator: gasoline up 27.4% y/y. Loses to **#4**.
- Reshoring / robotics integrator: manufacturing jobs are still about 1% below April 2025. Loses to **#4**.

- TikTok Shop / ecommerce / dropshipping: platform risk and saturation. Loses to **#1** **[judgment, not researched]**.
- Pressure washing: $5–15K to start, saturated. Loses to **#7** (same homeowner, about 10x the ticket).

- Landscaping: SDE about $188K. Loses to **#1** (HVAC SDE $315K).

- Bookkeeping / tax prep: AI eating the low end. Loses to **#8** **[judgment]**.

- Crypto / stablecoin plays: supply flat at $303–312B, GENIUS Act rules still unwritten. Loses to **#1**.
- Defense tech or humanoid-robotics startup: record VC money, but it needs VC-scale capital and follows a power law. Loses to **#2**.

**Gate D. Barrier bigger than the 5-year payoff**
- Elevator installer: $109,910 median, but only about 1,900 openings a year. Loses to **#4** (72,700 openings a year).
- Grid interconnection consulting: 2,060 GW waiting, but you need a licensed power engineer (PE).
- Dental practice: needs a DDS (dental degree).
- Starting an HVAC or plumbing shop from scratch: Texas licensing requires years of supervised experience. Loses to **#1** (buy the licensed shop).
- Welding: $53,750 median, 2% growth. Loses to **#4**.
- Childcare center: 46% of kids live in childcare deserts, but staff ratios and the roughly $13K/yr price ceiling squeeze margins. Loses to **#3**.
- Permit expediting: the bottleneck is city staff, and AI can't make the city faster. Loses to **#6**.
- NP / PA / sonographer: good jobs ($132K / $136K / $97K), but CRNA has the higher ceiling for the healthcare slot. Sonographer has the best pay per year of school if time matters more than ceiling.

**Gate E. Head-to-head near misses**
- Patient-side appeals / bill negotiation: patients pay $40–50 or nothing (Counterforce is free). Loses to **#8**.
- Unclaimed property finder: Texas caps finder fees at 10% and the state returns the money free. Loses to **#6**.
- Telecom / utility / freight bill audit: 25–50% contingency, but the error rates are vendor claims. Loses to **#6** on verified market size. **Close call.**
- AR-collections AI: QuickBooks and fintechs own it. Loses to **#8**.
- Public adjuster: capped at 10% in Texas and storm-dependent. Folded into **#7**.
- Hearing aids: adoption barely moved (38.4% → 39.1%).
- SMB cybersecurity MSP and backup-generator dealer: not researched this pass. Lesser on the evidence I have; these are open, not proven.

## Confidence

- **Target:** which 10 paths give the highest odds-weighted income for someone in the US starting in Sept 2026.
- **Well supported** (sourced): AI is still early (Census 17–20%); the electrician shortage and capex boom; aging demand; the rate environment.
- **Moderately supported:** HVAC trades at about 2.5x SDE while PE pays about 9.5x. These come from BizBuySell snippets because the pages blocked direct access.
- **Weakest:** traveling electrician pay (recruiter blogs), home care owner margins **[judgment]**, spam-remediation pricing (unverified).
- **Still open:** AI capex could be a bubble; debt-funded capex tripled as a share. If it pops, #2 and #4 dim while #1, #3 and #7 hold. The Fed is hiking, which raises #1's debt cost.
- **Load-bearing weakest claim:** that small service businesses still sell for about 2.5x SDE and SBA money is available at about 9.75%. If multiples rise or credit tightens after the new rules on 10/1/26, #1 falls below #2.
