The Paycheck Plan

What to buy with each paycheck, in order. About $25–40 a paycheck gets one adult to two weeks ready in eight paychecks.

Shopping list with links: preparedathome.netlify.app/paycheck-plan

Stage 172 hours Paychecks 1–4 · about $130 per adult

Paycheck 1 ~$25

  • 3 gallons of water per person$4
  • Manual can opener$5
  • Flashlight or headlamp + batteries$15

Paycheck 2 ~$30

  • 3 days of no-cook food: peanut butter, crackers, canned chicken or tuna, cereal, fruit cups$20
  • Paper plates, cups, utensils$5
  • Trash bags and zip-top bags$5

Paycheck 3 ~$35

  • First-aid kit$15
  • Pain reliever, allergy medicine$10
  • Toilet paper, wipes, hand sanitizer$10

Paycheck 4 ~$40

  • Battery or hand-crank radio (NOAA band)$25
  • Power bank + phone cable$15

Free, any week $0

  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
  • Photo or copy of IDs and insurance cards
  • Learn where your water and gas shut off
  • Fill clean bottles with tap water, label the date (page 8)
Stage 22 weeks Paychecks 5–8 · about $130 per adult

Paycheck 5 ~$30

  • 11 more gallons of water per person, or 2 refillable water jugs$15–25
  • Plain unscented bleach (page 8)$4

Paycheck 6 ~$30

  • Week 1 of shelf-stable food you already eat$25
  • Coffee, drink mixes, a comfort food$5

Paycheck 7 ~$35

  • Week 2 of shelf-stable food$25
  • Battery lantern$10

Paycheck 8 ~$35

  • 2 weeks of your Two-Week Count items (page 3)$20
  • Extra batteries or a second power bank$15
  • Ask your pharmacist about early refills$0
Can't spare $25? Do the free list first, then buy one line per paycheck. Stage 1 is 11 lines, so you still reach 72 hours in about five months of paychecks, and every item helps on its own.
Also on the Stage 1 list (page 4) if your home needs them: 7 days of prescriptions, pet food, baby supplies, spare glasses, a fire extinguisher.

After Stage 2: the rhythm

From here it's one habit, not a shopping list. Add a little to every grocery trip and let it build.

Stage 3 · 30 days Paychecks 9–14

  • Add $15–20 of staples to each grocery run: rice, dried beans, oats, pasta, canned meat
  • One paycheck: a water filter rated for bacteria and protozoa (page 6)
  • Buy 30 days of your Two-Week Count items (× 2), a few each trip
  • Start the pantry log (page 9)

Stage 4 · 90 days Paychecks 15–30

  • Same rhythm, $15–25 a paycheck, toward 90 days (× 6.5)
  • Sealed, pest-proof containers for bulk staples
  • Eat the oldest first, replace it on the next trip
  • Ask about 90-day refills for regular medicines
Rule of thumb: buy only what your household already eats and uses. A stocked pantry you rotate is worth more than a stack of food nobody likes.
