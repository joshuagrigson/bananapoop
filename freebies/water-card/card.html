The Water Card

How much to store, and how to make water safe when the tap isn't. Print it, tape it inside a cabinet door.

How much to store

  • 1 gallon per person, per day (drinking and basic hygiene).
  • 3 days minimum. 2 weeks at home if you can.
  • Add water for pets, and extra for nursing mothers, kids, the sick and hot weather.
  • Family of 4 for 2 weeks: 56 gallons.

Storing it right

  • Buy sealed bottled water, or fill clean food-grade containers.
  • Never use milk jugs or containers that held chemicals.
  • Keep it in a cool, dark place.
  • Replace water you bottled yourself every 6 months. Store-bought: by its date.

Boil first if you can

Bring clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 feet), let it cool, and store it in clean covered containers. Boiling kills germs that bleach can miss, like Giardia and Cryptosporidium.

When to stop and get help: if officials say the water is contaminated with fuel or chemicals, bleach and boiling won't fix it. Use bottled water and follow local instructions.
